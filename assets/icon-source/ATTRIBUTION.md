## Modifications
The geometry (four toe ellipses plus the pad outline) was redrawn as a clean vector, dropping the original leather-texture filter and artifacts. It was recoloured white to light pink (#fff to #ffd1ec), given a drop shadow, and composited on a purple gradient squircle with a glow and a glossy highlight. See `AppIcon-composite.svg` and `build_icon.py`, which generates the SVG, `AppIcon-1024.png` and `AppIcon.icns`.
